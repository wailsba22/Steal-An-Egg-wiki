Enter the pet weight. See the money.

Choose a pet, enter the weight shown at hatch, select its mutation, and get the estimated money per second.

Formula

How the estimate works

Weight changes the base income, then the mutation multiplier is applied.

Weighted income = base income × [1 + ((your weight ÷ average weight) − 1) × 0.5] × mutation × quantity.

Weight ranges and scaling are community estimates. Update the model in data.js when verified in-game.
