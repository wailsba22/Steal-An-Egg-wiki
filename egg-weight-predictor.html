How the estimate works

Rarity score = (egg weight − minimum rarity weight) ÷ (maximum rarity weight − minimum rarity weight).

The score is limited to 0–100%. The rarity menu is built from the pets currently listed in the selected area. A score or pet pool cannot guarantee a random hatch.

We are still collecting verified egg weights and hatch odds. Replace estimates in data.js as better data becomes available.
