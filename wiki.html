Biomes, guardians & speed gates

Biomes sit along a single route from your base outward. Each one is guarded by a creature that wakes up the moment you grab an egg from its nest — get caught in its home biome and the egg resets to the nest; get caught closer to base and it's still recoverable.

Pets & rarity

Ranked across ten tiers, from Common up to Divine. Income scales steeply near the top — the jump from Cosmic to Secret alone outweighs nearly everything below it combined.

Mutations

A pet's size sets its base income; a mutation then multiplies that number. Silver, Golden, and Rainbow can appear naturally on hatch or during Admin Abuse events. Bloom and Spirit Bloom only come from the Sakura Incubator, unlocked with a Crane from the Cherry Blossom biome.
